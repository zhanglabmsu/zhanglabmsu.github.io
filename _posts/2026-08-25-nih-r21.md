The [Liu Lab](https://www.xinyueliu.net/home) in Department of Chemical Engineering & Materials Science and the [Zhang Lab](https://zhanglabmsu.github.io/) in Department of Physiology received a mutliple-PI Trailblazer R21 award from the National Institute of Biomedical Imaging and Bioengineering (NIBIB) to develop a flexible, biocompatible optical fiber for efficient calcium signal detection in skeletal muscles of awake, free behaving animals. This technology and collaboration will expand our ability to investigate the physiological mechanisms that link activity in the brain and body.
