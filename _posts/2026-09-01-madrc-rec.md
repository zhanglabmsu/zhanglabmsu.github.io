Dr. Zhang has been selected as a awardee in the Michigan Alzheimer's Disease Research Center (MADRC) Early Career Investigator Mentorship Program, supported by a P30 grant from the National Institutes on Aging. This two-year support will expand his current research program in vascular physiology and healthy aging and strategically position his research to address cerebrovascular contributions to Alzheimer's disease.
