The [Jiang Lab](https://www.henryford.com/physician-directory/j/jiang-quan) in Department of Neurology at Henry Ford Health and the [Zhang Lab](https://zhanglabmsu.github.io/) in Department of Physiology at Michigan State University received a neuroscience seed funding from Henry Ford Health + Michigan State University Health Sciences to investigate brain waste clearance in the aging brain. For more information, see [here](https://henryfordmsu.org/news-and-events/news/2025/12/catalyzing-collaboration---2025-neuroscience-seed-funding-recipients).
