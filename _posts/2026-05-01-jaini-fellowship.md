Jaini Gandhi, a senior majoring in neuroscience and an undergraduate research assistant in our lab, recently won two prestigious fellowships to support her summer research and senior thesis work: the Hymen and Miriam Stein Scholarship from Honors College at Michigan State University and the Larry D. Fowler Endowment Undergraduate Research Scholarship from College of Natural Science at Michigan State University. Jaini will be working on optimizing pupil tracking algorithms and investigating the neurophysiology underlying motivated behaviors.
Congratulations, Jaini!
